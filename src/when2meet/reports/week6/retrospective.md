## What went well

- Sprint 4 delivered a usable Week 6 trial release: selected meeting time, room booking lifecycle, heatmap legend, and participant names.
- Customer reviewed README and `docs/customer-handover.md` and confirmed they match expectations.
- Customer-executed UAT from `docs/user-acceptance-tests.md` passed for the critical trial flows; customer stated the service works as expected.
- Presentation deck (3 SP) was finished in two days; several 1-SP items closed faster than estimated (37 SP total).
- Sprint Review, transition discussion, and documentation review fit into one short recorded session with a public transcript.

## What did not go well

- Legend placement was wrong on first delivery (bottom instead of top).
- Selected-time clear bug appeared only during the live demo.
- External room-booking outage interrupted part of the live verification.
- Not every team member attended the customer session.

## What we changed compared to the previous Sprint retrospective

- Week 5 asked to treat “approved with changes” outcomes as open PBIs immediately — Sprint 4 converted legend and booking follow-ups into closed PBIs and left placement/mobile items explicitly for Sprint 5.
- Customer-facing documentation was treated as a first-class Sprint deliverable rather than a late report attachment.

## Process improvements for the next Sprint

1. **Smoke the exact demo path on mobile and desktop the morning of Review**, including clear/reset actions.
2. **Have a fallback recording or stub for external Room Booking** so a third-party outage does not block Review evidence.
3. **Close documentation acceptance in writing** (as done this week) before claiming transition readiness.
