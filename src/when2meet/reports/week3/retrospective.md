## What went well

- The team prepared an initial product backlog and Sprint roadmap, giving the Sprint a shared delivery scope.
- Core MVP work progressed toward enabling meeting creation, invitation sharing, and participant availability collection.
- Customer feedback was gathered during the Sprint, providing input for refining the next iteration.

## What did not go well

- Task tracking was not maintained consistently, making it difficult to see ownership and current progress across the team.
- The customer meeting was scheduled at a time that was not convenient for all team members, which limited team attendance.
- Several tasks remained unassigned until the final day of the Sprint, reducing the time available for implementation and review.

## Action points

- Assign every Sprint task on the first day, set clear internal deadlines, and review task status in a short regular progress check.
- Schedule customer and team meetings earlier, using an availability check to select a time that works for the whole team.
